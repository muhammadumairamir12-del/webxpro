Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 12, 2026

At SkyEagle Studio, we value the trust you place in us to build your digital solutions. This Privacy Policy details how we handle, process, and safeguard the information you provide when using our website and lead submission features.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an inquiry form or initiate a WhatsApp chat, we collect details necessary to scope your project proposal, including:

  • Your full name and contact email address.
  • Your phone or WhatsApp number.
  • Company name and target project category.
  • Budget specifications and project details description.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used solely to evaluate system parameters and coordinate communication regarding your project proposal. We do not sell, rent, or distribute your email or WhatsApp number to marketing lists or third-party advertising grids.

3. Cookies and Diagnostics

Our website is built on a clean static HTML codebase. We do not place invasive tracking scripts or behavioral cookies. We use standard hosting telemetry logs (such as page speed counters and access logs) to monitor server health and security.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions about our privacy guidelines or want your details scrubbed from our workspace registries, contact us at: infoskyeagle12@gmail.com.
